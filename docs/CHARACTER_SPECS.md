# Character Visual Specifications — THE LAST BELL (Chapter 01)

Reference Lineup Sheet: `/src/assets/images/ref_characters_lineup_1794832133584.jpg`

All six students wear **Orion Academy** autumn/winter uniform pieces (dark navy blazer `#162238` with subtle academy crest on left chest, crisp off-white dress shirt `#E2E8F0`, striped burgundy/slate tie `#7F1D1D`/`#475569`, charcoal trousers or pleated skirt `#334155`), personalized cleanly to telegraph personality and silhouette immediately.

---

## 1. Samer (سامر) — Player Protagonist
- **Age / Role:** 17 · Observant investigator.
- **Height / Build:** Medium height (`1.00×` baseline), lean agile posture.
- **Silhouette & Clothing:** Unbuttoned navy blazer over slightly untucked crisp shirt, loosened tie, dark slate trousers, plain dark leather shoes. Hands relaxed at sides, alert forward stance.
- **Hairstyle & Features:** Slightly messy dark-brown/black textured hair falling near eyebrows, sharp observant dark eyes, calm neutral brow.
- **Color Accent:** Cool slate & muted charcoal (`#1E293B`).
- **Chapter 01 Expressions:** `neutral`, `worried` (narrowed concerned brow), `angry` (tight jaw, sharp eyes), `sad` (downcast gaze), plus `speaking` state.

## 2. Layan (ليان) — Analytical Ally
- **Age / Role:** 17 · Quiet, detail-oriented, excels at math.
- **Height / Build:** Slightly shorter (`0.94×` baseline), upright composed posture.
- **Silhouette & Clothing:** Neat buttoned blazer, charcoal trousers, small notebook held at chest/side in world pose.
- **Hairstyle & Features:** Straight shoulder-length chestnut-brown hair tucked cleanly behind one ear, thin rectangular dark-slate glasses.
- **Color Accent:** Cool muted steel blue (`#64748B`).
- **Identifying Feature:** Glasses + clutched investigation/math notebook.
- **Chapter 01 Expressions:** `neutral`, `worried`, `angry`, `sad`, plus `speaking`.

## 3. Yacine (ياسين) — Dominant & Defensive
- **Age / Role:** 18 · Wealthy family, tries to lead/control the room, hides panic about Elena.
- **Height / Build:** Tallest student (`1.07×` baseline), broad shoulders, confident weight-shifted stance (one hand in pocket).
- **Silhouette & Clothing:** Tailored navy blazer with crisp collar and perfectly knotted tie, leather belt, neatly pressed slate trousers.
- **Hairstyle & Features:** Wavy dark-brown swept-back hair, defined jawline, slightly guarded expression.
- **Color Accent:** Deep burgundy & silver-gray (`#881337`).
- **Identifying Feature:** Taller frame + impeccably tailored posture that stiffens visibly during Elena mentions.
- **Chapter 01 Expressions:** `neutral`, `worried` (sweat/tense brow), `angry` (defensive glare), `sad` (averted eyes), plus `speaking`.

## 4. Nour (نور) — Guarded Cheerfulness
- **Age / Role:** 17 · Uses humor to mask nerves; drops water bottle when Elena's photo appears.
- **Height / Build:** Short-medium (`0.93×` baseline), expressive open posture.
- **Silhouette & Clothing:** Academy blazer draped casually over her shoulders like a cape, short-sleeved white shirt, pleated skirt, knee-high dark socks, burgundy neck scarf/ribbon variation.
- **Hairstyle & Features:** Wavy dark-black chin-length bob with soft bangs, bright expressive eyes.
- **Color Accent:** Soft muted rose-burgundy (`#9F1239`).
- **Identifying Feature:** Draped shoulder blazer + bob cut; water bottle prop on floor/desk after Scene 02 reveal.
- **Chapter 01 Expressions:** `neutral` (forced half-smile), `worried` (wide startled eyes), `angry` (defensive frown), `sad` (hollow quiet stare), plus `speaking`.

## 5. Karim (كريم) — Guarded Tech Specialist
- **Age / Role:** 17 · Shy, sharp with electronics, exits at Chapter 01 climax via tablet summons.
- **Height / Build:** Medium-slim (`0.97×` baseline), slightly hunched protective posture.
- **Silhouette & Clothing:** Charcoal knit sweater vest beneath navy blazer, white sneakers instead of dress loafers, over-ear matte black headphones resting around his neck, glowing **school tablet** held in hands.
- **Hairstyle & Features:** Straight black hair with neat fringe, square-framed glasses.
- **Color Accent:** Faint pale cyan screen glow (`#7DD3FC` subtle reflection only, never neon cyberpunk).
- **Identifying Feature:** Neck headphones + school tablet (consistent with Canon Decision D4).
- **Chapter 01 Expressions:** `neutral`, `worried`, `angry`, `sad`, plus `speaking`.

## 6. Mariam (مريم) — Direct & Unyielding
- **Age / Role:** 18 · Strong-willed, Elena's former friend before an unresolved fallout.
- **Height / Build:** Medium-tall (`0.98×` baseline), firm stance with arms crossed.
- **Silhouette & Clothing:** Structured blazer with rolled/crisp cuffs, pleated slate skirt, dark socks and loafers.
- **Hairstyle & Features:** Long dark hair pulled firmly into a high sleek ponytail, sharp arched eyebrows.
- **Color Accent:** Cold iron-gray (`#475569`).
- **Identifying Feature:** High ponytail + crossed-arms silhouette.
- **Chapter 01 Expressions:** `neutral`, `worried`, `angry`, `sad`, plus `speaking`.

## 7. Adam Mourad (آدم مراد) — Former Philosophy & History Teacher (Ch01 Scope Only)
- **Age / Role:** 39 · Elena's older brother, calm orchestrator appearing via projector broadcast.
- **Visual Identity:** Crisp tailored dark charcoal coat over black turtleneck or dark buttoned shirt, silver-streaked dark hair combed neatly back, tired yet piercing eyes, cool rim lighting with subtle projector scanlines.
- **Chapter 01 Assets Needed:** Broadcast portrait (`neutral/calm` + `serious/intense` frame) framed inside projector screen feed only (no full walkable sprite needed in Ch01).

## 8. Elena Mourad (إيلينا مراد) — Deceased Student (Ch01 Scope Only)
- **Age / Role:** 17 (3 years ago) · Quiet, artistic honor student. Treated as a real human presence, never a horror ghost.
- **Visual Identity:** Gentle, expressive dark eyes, soft shoulder-length dark hair with a simple hairpin, wearing the same Orion Academy uniform from three years ago. Warm-faded archival monochrome/sepia-slate portrait treatment.
- **Chapter 01 Assets Needed:** Single archival projector portrait + small miniature presence inside the cut group photograph (no dialogue sprite needed in Ch01).
